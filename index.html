02 — Focus方向

Research研究

01

Diffusion acceleration扩散加速

Training-free trajectory search and feature caching, so denoising spends compute where it changes the image. Representative work: LoReS and LinCa. 用免训练的轨迹筛选和特征缓存，把去噪计算花在真正改变图像的地方。代表工作是 LoReS 和 LinCa。

LoReS
02

Dynamic tokens动态 token

High-resolution computation goes to tokens that are semantically important and still changing. Representative work: AViTS and SpecEdit. 高分辨率计算只分给语义关键、并且仍在变化的 token。代表工作是 AViTS 和 SpecEdit。

AViTS
03

Editing and video编辑与视频

The same idea extends past text-to-image. SpecEdit reaches up to 10× on Qwen-Image-Edit, and LinCa also accelerates video models. 加速不限于文生图。SpecEdit 在 Qwen-Image-Edit 上最高约 10 倍，LinCa 也覆盖视频模型。

SpecEdit
04

Visual reasoning视觉推理

A task-centered survey with NTU MMLab: which state a visual task needs, and where a generative model’s reasoning actually comes from. Sole first author. 和 NTU MMLab 一起整理视觉任务需要什么样的状态，以及生成模型的推理能力从哪里来。独立一作综述。

Survey综述
